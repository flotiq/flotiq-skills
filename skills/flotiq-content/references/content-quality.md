# Content quality

Guidance for writing content — real or sample/seed — that reads as genuine rather than generated filler.

## Realistic sample content

* Write content that resembles what a real editor for this domain would write: real-sounding names, plausible prices, believable dates, domain-appropriate terminology.
* Avoid tells of generated filler: "Sample Product 1," "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet," repeated sentence templates across every object, sequential/numbered placeholder names.
* Vary sentence structure, length, and specific details across a batch — five products described with the same three-sentence template read as fake even if the words differ.
* Ground details in the project's actual domain. A bookstore's sample products should sound like books (titles, authors, genres), not generic "Product A / Product B."

## Consistent tone and style

* Before writing new content, sample a few existing objects (if any) and match their register: formal vs. casual, sentence length, use of second person, terminology choices (e.g. "customers" vs. "users").
* Within a single batch of new content, keep the voice consistent object-to-object — it should read as if one person wrote all of it.
* Keep factual details (dates, names, numbers) internally consistent across related objects — a blog post's `publishedAt` should make sense relative to its author's bio, a product's price should be plausible for its category.

## Volume and coverage

* When asked for "sample content" without a specific count, prefer a small but complete, well-formed set over a large volume of thin objects — quality over quantity unless a specific number is requested.
* Cover the range of states the schema supports (e.g. some published, some draft, if that's realistic for the use case) rather than making every object identical in state.

## Verifying quality, not just presence

Populating a field is not the same as populating it well. Before considering content done, re-read it as an editor would: does it make sense, is it free of placeholder text, does it match the rest of the project?
